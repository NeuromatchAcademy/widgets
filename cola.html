About our fees

At Neuromatch, our mission is to democratize education and research in the computational sciences globally. Course fees go directly to sustaining the Academy and supporting the Teaching Assistants (TAs) who dedicate an entire month to teaching you. Without them, Neuromatch could not operate. Read more about Neuromatch Academy.

To keep courses accessible, we adjust fees by region using GDP per capita data from the World Bank, so participants from many socioeconomic and geographical backgrounds can join.

Most professors and universities pay these fees directly from grants or education funds. Use our Tuition Toolkit to start the conversation with your supervisor about covering the cost of the course.

If you can’t get reimbursed after exploring all available options, we reduce your course fee by 50%. In exceptional circumstances, if you can’t afford the reduced fee, you can apply for an additional hardship discount through the payment portal.

A non-refundable processing fee, also adjusted by region, covers administrative costs. See our Course Refund Policy.
