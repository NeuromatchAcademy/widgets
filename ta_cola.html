TA pay calculator

TA pay is adjusted by location. Choose your country of residence and the course you’re TAing to see your pay.

Pay transparency

Regional pay adjustments are based on GDP per capita data from the World Bank. GDP per capita is a flawed measure that leaves out wealth inequality, but it’s the best indicator we’ve found for making pay more equitable and accessible across countries and regions, one of the core tenets of Neuromatch Academy.

Half of your pay is the same for everyone. The other half is adjusted by your country’s regional rate. Amounts shown are gross pay in US dollars.
