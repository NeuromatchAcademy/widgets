TA pay is set by region. Choose your country of residence and the course you’re TAing to see your pay.

Pay transparency

We set TA pay by region using GDP per capita data from the World Bank. It’s an imperfect measure that doesn’t capture wealth inequality, but it’s the best indicator we’ve found for making pay more equitable and accessible across countries and regions, one of the core tenets of Neuromatch Academy.

Part of every TA’s pay is the same wherever they live, and part is set by region.
